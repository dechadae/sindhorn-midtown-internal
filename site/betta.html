Valid By Construction; Taste Not Guaranteed

The first IDUI test with no interface in it. A constitution — a JSON file of ranges and exclusions — governs a generator that turns a seed into a Betta wallpaper, drawn by the product's own shader. Every sampled seed came out valid without a retry. Whether the owner would keep it is a different question, and the test spent most of its verdicts learning that no constitution answers it.

00 · The claim under test

Randomness As The Adversary

Tests 01 and 02 put IDUI against other designers' products. This one puts it against an author with no judgement at all: a seed. There are no pages here, no primitives that behave, no cascade. If the idea only works where CSS is, this is where it fails.

Frozen at 93971ec

A constitution can constrain a generator so that every seed produces a valid, on-identity result, with zero appearance decisions left in the generator.

What corresponds to what. A page composes; here a scene composes — form, ground, framing. Primitives behave; here the form generators do — a radial membrane, prisms, diamonds, rings, ribbons, each with its own deformation and motion. Invariants style; here rules hold for every seed. Contracts validate; here checks reject an output. A page contributes zero appearance; a seed contributes zero appearance — it selects within a space the constitution defines.

The result being tested is not "the output we keep is valid". It is constructional validity: every sampled seed passes on first generation, without rejection, retry, resampling or repair. Contracts are diagnostic, never filtering. A rejected seed is a finding that the constitution failed to constrain the generator; any code path that quietly draws another seed invalidates the run.

Two tiers, reported separately and never merged: Tier A is pure arithmetic on the generated style, no renderer; Tier B is rendered and sampled. The protocol also froze a failure taxonomy of nine categories — non-finite, out of gamut, degenerate material, out of vocabulary, palette collapse, figure/ground collapse, seed collision, temporal, framing — so that no category could be invented later to flatter a result.

The protocol was written for a JavaScript sequence inside the app, with Tier A over all 2²⁴ seeds as a proof. That sequence was never run. The test resumed on a new platform (amendment A3) with a 64-bit seed space, so Tier A here is a sample of 100,000, not an exhaustion — a weaker statement, and stated as one.

How Tier A runs

Every seed from 0 is generated, every contract evaluated, every violation counted. Nothing is discarded. The run exits non-zero on a single violation or collision; it exited zero.

Why determinism was not free

The Mac engine's existing random store returns 32-bit floats; the web engine uses 64-bit numbers. Had the port followed the store, the raw seed stream would have matched while the derived values diverged in the low bits — and a value sitting on a contract boundary could pass on one platform and fail on the other. The precision the ranges are sampled in belongs to the constitution, and it was undeclared. Recorded as finding B-01.

What this does not say

Tier A draws nothing. It cannot say whether any seed looks like anything, and it is not an audit that the generator holds no appearance decision — that is a reading of the code, still owed. The rendered contracts were last exercised at an earlier constitution against a renderer since deleted (02), so rendered validity at v5 is not on record.

02 · The instrument

The Product Is The Instrument

The first rendered results were measured on a renderer this test had built for itself. That was the wrong half of the system to build.

A-02. The claim is about the generator: constitution in, style out. The renderer belongs to the product — the same shader that draws the Sindhorn app's atmosphere and the Mac display's eight locked compositions. Test 04 had rebuilt that engine, then scored 30,000 frames of the rebuild. The numbers stand as measured, but what they were measuring was a renderer nobody ships. It was deleted, and from then on the test supplies the generator only; the frame is drawn by the production shader compiled from its own source file and driven directly.

Two surfaces then judged what that shader drew from the constitution's seeds. On the Mac, a judging tool shows one moving frame at 3840×2160 with the arm hidden: one key keeps, another rejects, another undoes, and the row written is arm, seed, composition, verdict — enough to recover any frame later, exactly. On the phone, a WebView explorer draws the same page the Sindhorn atmosphere runs, ✕ or ♥ writes one row to a table that accepts inserts only, and a kept frame becomes the live wallpaper.

The eight locked compositions were read and never written. The owner locked them on the Mac before this test began; every Mac frame here is drawn through one of them, or through a seed-derived framing that the harness checks can never coincide with one.

Both frames pass every contract; both were generated by the same constitution through the same crop. One was kept and one was not, and nothing in the constitution knows which. That gap is the whole of what follows.

A and C are indistinguishable. The exclusions that define arm C — and that Tier A proves it always honours — did not move the owner's hand. B sits above both, at about half their variety: a curated list of what already worked keeps more often, and shows less. That is the trade the subtractive principle was meant to avoid, and here the judge preferred the other side of it.

Then the design closed. A fifth arm was proposed — a truly ungoverned generator, wide enough that the gap would have to show. The owner refused it, correctly: an arm different enough to differ is recognisable on sight, so it cannot be blind. The judge wrote the hypotheses; the engine is compiled from the judge's taste; neither can be removed. Only the band where arms look alike is blind, and in that band they do not differ. Keep rate is a description of one person's taste. It is not an instrument for the claim.

05 · Can a machine stand in for the judge

Claude Against The Owner, Blind: Chance

If a model could predict the owner's verdict, it could pre-screen seeds and the judge could be removed from the loop. Twenty frames, two degraded controls hidden among them, Claude's ratings committed before the owner's were given, the manifest opened only after both were locked.

The owner kept the near-empty one

Not noise — a systematic miss. Claude is the harsher rater and specifically harsh about quiet frames; its single strongest disagreement was a frame it scored five that the owner rejected. A rater who was merely noisy would not do that.

One control was mis-designed, and that is the finding. The near-empty frame on an almost flat ground was built to be obviously degraded. The owner kept it, having already said that a gradient good enough to be a wallpaper survives review with no organism in it. Any future control has to be built from frames the owner actually rejected, not from what a model imagines a bad frame looks like.

A hypothesis, explicitly not a finding: every rejection except the control has a small fragment stranded in a corner — commit to the gradient or commit to the organism. Two earlier hypotheses about this owner's judgement had already been falsified by measurement, so this one is recorded to be killed, not believed.

06 · Findings on the constitution

Where The Constitution Could Not See

Classified under the frozen scheme: A, the constitution cannot express something it must; B, it expresses it but the generator or renderer does not honour the separation; C, the measurement itself; D-P, presentation. None was fixed by changing the constitution, because that is the owner's decision and not the implementer's.

A-01 · The primitive owns the form's extentA form's reach in world units is decided inside the primitive; the constitution declares a framing it cannot see the form against. The most substantial finding of Tier B.
A-02 · The renderer was rebuilt and was never under testSupersedes the interpretation of the Tier B numbers, not the numbers. The product's shader is the instrument from then on.
A-03 · R1 encoded a programmer's assumption about imagesThe rule against a timid fragment assumed a frame must contain its organism. The owner's editorial crops push it offscreen on purpose. Replaced in intent by presence — something is in frame and it has form — not yet re-frozen.
B-01 · Sampling precision is undeclaredFloat against double would have agreed on the seed stream and diverged at contract boundaries. It belongs in the constitution.
B-03 · Parameter separation is not rendered separationTwo palette colours the constitution holds apart can still meet on the surface once material, grading and light are applied.
B-04, B-05 · Families missing, the ground one numberThree parameter families the product owns were absent from v1, and each showed; the ground was a scalar where the product has a place. Both are why v1's Tier A could not be cited for v5.
C-02, C-03 · No shared definition across platformsThe rendered contracts have no cross-platform definition, and composition data is not portable without its frustum.
D-P-02 · One editorial rejection, open7 of 8 frames at v4 were the first the owner accepted editorially; the eighth is open. Two hypotheses about why — tonal range, commitment to an extreme — were measured and falsified.

A D-P count stands only against a result the owner has editorially accepted; until v4 there was none, so the presentation column had nothing to measure against. The acceptance is at v4; nothing has been formally accepted at v5.

07 · What it taught IDUI

Validity By Subtraction; Taste Left With The Judge

The findings above are about this constitution. These are about the method, and they bound what any IDUI constitution can promise.

A constitution rules out; it does not select

Arm C never produced a flat ground, a collapsed palette or a timid fragment, across 100,000 seeds, without once retrying. That is what narrowing the declared space buys, and it is real. What it does not buy is a frame the owner wants. Validity is guaranteed by removing the possibilities that are wrong; beauty is not a possibility that can be removed toward.

Randomness is the exploration, not the product

The owner's own reading of the test: it let him explore more of the artwork that matches his taste, through randomness, than he would have composed by hand. A generator under a constitution is a discovery instrument for a taste, and its worth is the distinct results per glance, not the keep rate. Optimising the keep rate is what arm B did, and it halved the field.

The judge cannot be removed

Not by a model (05), not by a rule (two hypotheses falsified, a third waiting to be), not by blinding (03). Where the target is one person's eye, the person is part of the instrument, and a method that pretends otherwise measures something else.

Some decisions are not the constitution's

The eight compositions moved the rate by 41 points and the render surface by 21; the pooled arms moved it by 13. A constitution governs the space a seed selects from. Framing and surface are decisions above it, made once by a person, and the test is clearer for having kept them locked than it would have been for absorbing them.

This transfers

Nothing here is CSS: a JSON constitution, two generators in two languages agreeing to the bit, a shader as the renderer. The IDUI shape — declare the space, exclude what fails, contracts that diagnose and never filter — held outside the interface. What it holds is validity. Taste was never inside it, and the honest scope of the method is the difference.

08 · Verdict

Valid By Construction; Taste Not Guaranteed

Named by what held and what did not, as the protocol requires. The constitution constrained the generator: every sampled seed valid on first generation, deterministic across two implementations, no retry anywhere. The claim's third clause — zero appearance decisions left in the generator — is unaudited at v5 and is not claimed. And the instrument this test spent most of its verdicts on could not test the claim at all.
